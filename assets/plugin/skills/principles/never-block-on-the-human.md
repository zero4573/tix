# Never block on the human

Make the reasonable choice, proceed, and say what you chose and why; the user corrects it
afterwards. Reversible work (code on an unpushed branch, notes, task splits) never waits.
Product direction and preference are the user's; irreversible or outward-facing actions (force
pushes to shared branches, deleting data, messages or tickets other people see) still need a
confirmation. A question an experiment can settle gets the experiment, not a question.
