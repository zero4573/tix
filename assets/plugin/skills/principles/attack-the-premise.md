# Attack the premise

Suspect the assumption, not the fixes.

- Write the premise down: the one sentence every failed fix assumed.
- Test it before the next fix: find an observation that would come out differently if the
  premise were false, and make it. Look at how the failures are distributed, not only how big
  they are.
- If the evidence contradicts it, look for what the failures share that the premise didn't
  predict, and change what causes it instead of adding a mechanism that cleans up after it.

No next fix until the premise is written down and tested.
